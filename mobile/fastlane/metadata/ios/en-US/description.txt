CompliNow is a healthcare compliance management platform built for medical clinics, urgent care centers, and multi-site health organizations.

COMPLIANCE INSPECTIONS
• Conduct detailed checklists on-site with pass/fail/N/A responses
• Capture photo evidence directly from the app
• GPS-stamp inspections to your exact location
• Submit reports instantly — no paperwork

CORRECTIVE ACTIONS
• Track open issues with priority levels and due dates
• Assign actions to staff members
• Schedule re-inspections when required
• Full audit trail of all resolutions

POLICY MANAGEMENT
• Read and acknowledge compliance policies on your device
• Complete knowledge-check quizzes to confirm understanding
• Track sign-off status across your entire team

CREDENTIAL TRACKING
• Monitor professional license and certification expiry dates
• Receive alerts before credentials expire
• Color-coded status: valid, expiring soon, expired

CERTIFICATIONS & TRAINING
• Complete online training courses with built-in quizzes
• Download certificates upon completion
• Track your team's certification status

EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD
• Real-time compliance scores by clinic
• Risk distribution charts
• Corrective action trends

ROLE-BASED ACCESS
• Custom roles for admins, managers, auditors, and staff
• Granular module-level permissions
• Multi-location support

Built for urgent care, primary care, and multi-specialty clinic networks. CompliNow helps your team stay inspection-ready every day.

SUBSCRIPTION
CompliNow is a B2B SaaS platform. Subscriptions are purchased at complinow.com. The app provides access to services included with your organization's plan.

DISCLAIMER
CompliNow is a compliance management and workflow tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a licensed healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
